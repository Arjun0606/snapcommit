The realistic path. Not optimistic, not pessimistic. Just the work.

## Week 1 — Launch week

**Day 1: Show HN + PH simultaneously.**

Goal: 50-200 signups across the 48-hour window. Realistic. If you front-page HN, double that.

Conversion expectations:
- HN visitors: ~3,000-8,000 if front page (top 5)
- HN visitor → demo trial: ~15-25%
- Demo trial → subscriber: ~3-7%
- So front-page HN typically yields 50-150 paid signups in week 1.

If you don't front-page HN, you're at 20-60 signups in launch week. Still a real number.

**Day 1-7: Engagement, not new code.**
- Reply to every HN/PH comment within 1 hour, 8am-10pm
- DM every signup a thank-you and ask what's working/broken
- Fix bugs from real usage within 24h
- Tweet 1-2 times daily: feedback you got, screenshots, gratitude
- Submit the blog post to indie hackers and dev.to mid-week

**Week 1 target:** 50-150 paid signups = $450-$1,350 MRR

## Weeks 2-4 — Stabilize + start the content engine

The hardest weeks. Launch traffic dries up. You're back to organic.

**Daily (Mon-Fri):**
- 1 substantive Reddit/Twitter/HN comment in an AI memory / dev tools thread
- 1 reply to user feedback
- Fix 1 bug or polish 1 rough edge
- Check analytics for unusual signups or drops

**Weekly:**
- 1 blog post or tweet thread (rotate: technical deep-dives, user-story, comparison, philosophy)
- 1 outreach DM to a relevant influencer/podcast
- Review last 7 days: signups, conversion rate, top referrer

**Content topics for the first month:**
- "Why we're not a database" (already drafted)
- "Captures rejections, not just decisions" (the differentiator)
- "Memory across 30+ MCP clients: what we shipped"
- "The Supabase + Dodo solo-dev stack"
- "Live demo on landing page: should every paid-only SaaS have one?"

**Week 4 target:** $1,500-3,000 MRR (with consistent execution)

---

## Weeks 5-8 — Compound the content + community

Focus shifts from "tell people about Snapcommit" to "be useful to the AI/MCP community."

**Daily:**
- Respond to Reddit threads about AI memory pain — not pitching, just helpfully mentioning Snapcommit when relevant
- Engage on AI-builder Twitter
- Update GitHub README and docs based on real questions

**Weekly:**
- 1 deep-dive blog post (1500+ words on architecture, design decisions, lessons learned)
- 1 community post (indie hackers, dev.to, hashnode)
- 1 user spotlight (with permission) — story of how someone uses Snapcommit
- 1 minor product polish based on user feedback

**Specific outreach week 5-8:**
- Lenny's Newsletter pitch (it's a long shot but they cover dev tools)
- Theo (t3.gg) podcast tease
- Hacker News "Ask HN" or "Tell HN" follow-up if you have new traction to share
- Show your support for any Cursor/Claude Code-adjacent founders' launches (community first)

**Week 8 target:** $3,000-4,500 MRR

---

## Weeks 9-12 — Push to $5K MRR

This is where retention and upsells matter as much as new signups.

**Retention focus:**
- Identify cohorts that churn fastest — make sure they get a personal email
- Add features that make Snapcommit indispensable in week 1 of a new subscription (e.g., better onboarding, automatic project detection, daily digest)
- Tighten the quota nudge so 30% of Hobby users hit Pro by month 2

**Upsell:**
- Hobby → Pro conversion. Need to make Pro worth $20 more.
- Soft email to active Hobby users at 80% quota: "next tier gives you 10x"

**New surfaces:**
- Better dashboard at snapcommit.com/dashboard (let users see their memory inventory)
- Slack/Discord community if you have 100+ paying users
- A "Snapcommit Skills" library — opinionated starter packs for specific project types

**Week 12 target:** $4,000-6,000 MRR

## Daily metrics you actually track

A single Google Sheet (or use Plausible's free tier). Track each Sunday night:

| Metric | Target by week 12 |
|--------|-------------------|
| Landing page visits / week | 2,000-5,000 |
| Demo trials / week | 200-800 |
| Demo → subscriber conversion | 3-7% |
| Paid signups / week | 15-40 |
| Free-to-paid time (visits to sub) | <14 days median |
| Monthly churn % | <5% |
| MRR | $4,000-$6,000 |

If at week 4 demo conversion is <1%, the messaging is wrong. Iterate.
If at week 8 signup count is <30/week consistently, the distribution channels are wrong. Add channels.
If at week 12 churn is >10%, retention is wrong. Fix the onboarding.

## The honest read on $5K MRR

It's achievable in 90 days IF:
1. The launch goes well enough to seed 50+ users (5K MRR needs roughly 350-500 users total, mix of tiers)
2. You sustain 1 blog post + daily engagement consistently for 12 weeks
3. Churn stays under 10% month over month
4. You don't pivot product, pivot price, pivot positioning in the middle

It's $0 in 90 days IF:
1. You ship the launch broken
2. You go quiet after launch week
3. You build new features instead of talking to users
4. You hide from negative feedback

Energy management matters more than execution speed.

Do the work daily. Keep going when traction is quiet. The first 90 days are the steepest part of any curve — almost everyone who quits indie SaaS does so in the first 90 days, right before the inflection.
